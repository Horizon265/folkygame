# folkygame
folkygame is a python based game made by using the principles of menu driven programs 
It functions like a linguistic horror quiz where it tests the linguistic knowledge of a person while also catering to the horror vibe of the spooktober season.
while I was at boba bash delhi this game came in my mind as i was recently practicing menu driven programs in python.

*To summarise it all folkygame is a python game operating on the principles of a menu driven program it is a linguistic quiz based game which caters to the halloween spirit while also checking the linguistic knowledge of a person made by me at boba bash delhi for submission in the 3 a.m. hackclub program.

THE GAME TAKES PLACE IN 4 STAGES ALL FILLED WITH THEIR FAIR SHARE OF SPOOKINESS:-

* STAGE 1:- THE WORD:-
THE FIRST STAGE MAKES THE PLAYER GUESS THE WORD THAT HAS BEEN ENGRAVED INTO THE WORLD OF HORROR BY ONE OF THE MOST POPULAR AUTHORS IN THE GENRE AND ALSO ONE WHO REDEFINED WHAT HORROR MEANT FOR THE WORLD.

* STAGE 2:-POETRY
 STAGE 2 GIVES THE PLAYER  ONE OF THE MOST FAMOUS POETRY EXTRACTS IN ALL OF HORROR AND ASKS THE PLAYER TO GUESS THE POET 

*STAGE 3:-NOVEL:-
STAGE 3 ASKS PLAYER ABOUT WHAT IS POTENTIALLY THE MOST FAMOUS NOVEL IN ALL OF HORROR FAMOUS FOR IT'S ROLE IN BRINGING CERTAIN CREATURES OF HORROR INTO THE POP CULTURE OF MODERN DAY

*STAGE 4:-MACBETH:-
THE GAME ASKS THE PLAYER TO IDENTIFY THE PLAYWRIGHT FOR MACBETH A TRAGIC BUT WORLDWIDE FAMOUS PLAY FOR IT'S DEPTH AND STORYTELLING
